# User Stories

Core experience stories that define the Nextplace product.

## Core Constraints

All stories adhere to these constraints:
- **GPS location** - No location input, device provides it
- **4 hours minimum notice** - Nothing suggested sooner
- **Named social proof** - "Sarah is going" not "12 others"
- **Binary commitment** - Join or don't, no defer/snooze
- **10am-10pm availability** - MVP assumes open schedule
- **Zero user input** - System decides, user shows up

---

## Stories

### 1. The Suggestion
"Thursday night, one thing appears: 'Fly a kite at Ocean Beach tomorrow. Wind is 15mph, perfect conditions. Meet at the north parking lot, free parking. 10am-12pm.' No options, no browsing. Sometimes there's no listed event - the app constructs one: 'Explore the murals in Balmy Alley. 45 minutes, self-guided.' Place and activity, paired."

### 2. Named Social Proof
"I open the app and see: 'Sarah is going to San Jose Animal Shelter tomorrow morning.' One button: 'Join her.' Not '12 others' - a name. Someone real."

### 3. Weather Intelligence
"It's 95° at home. The app shows: '68° and foggy, 35 minutes west.' Distance and conditions, not city names. When Saturday rained out, it pivoted to an indoor shelter slot by 2pm. The app waits for good weather, and adapts when weather changes."

### 4. Flow 3 Unlock
"Social events were grayed out until I completed my first solo outing. Then they appeared. The system doesn't trust me with others until I've proven I'll show up alone."

### 5. Commitment Weight
"When I tapped 'Join', a screen said: 'Maria is counting on you. Be at Guadalupe Trail by 10am Saturday.' I can only commit to two events at once. The system has quiet rules - it enforces seriousness without lecturing."

### 6. Small Groups & Slots
"The event had 4 people and 1 spot left. I joined, it filled. Not a crowd, not a date. 2-6 people, just enough to have a conversation while walking. When it's full, others find different events."

### 7. Host Accountability
"I browsed locations and tapped 'Check it out' on a trailhead. I was the host now. The event appeared for others to join. I had to show up - hosts don't flake."

### 8. Practical Details
"The suggestion included everything: 'Walk the South Rim Trail at Alum Rock. 3 miles, moderate. Meet at the fountain by the rose garden. Free parking at north lot. 10am-12pm, 2 hours.' The coffee spot after was marked '$'. I knew what I was getting into."

### 9. Arrival & Presence
"I was driving to the trailhead. The host's phone buzzed: 'Jim is arriving shortly.' She knew to wait by the fountain. I missed a different event without canceling once - nothing was said, but my next suggestion took longer to arrive."

### 10. Post-Event
"The event ended at noon. At 1pm, my phone asked how it went. I rated the experience and each person separately. Someone was rude - I left a short note to the admins with my rating. Two of us gave each other 5 stars - the app asked if we wanted to be friends. Someone I rated poorly vanished from future suggestions. Quiet consequences."

### 11. Two-Day Window
"I couldn't join an event 3 days out - too far. I couldn't join one starting in 2 hours - too soon. The app lives in a 4-hour to 48-hour window. Spontaneous but not impulsive, soon but not someday."

### 12. Dog-Friendly
"I checked 'I have a dog' in settings. Now every suggestion shows a paw icon - dog-friendly trails, dog-friendly patios. One checkbox, and the system adapted."

### 13. Venue Attributes
"The post-hike hangout card showed icons: serves food, no alcohol, dog-friendly patio. The brewery downtown showed: serves alcohol, no dogs. I knew which crowd to expect."

### 14. Activity Diversity
"One week: trail cleanup. Next: outdoor yoga in the park (mats provided). Then: golden hour photography walk. The app rotates through activities I can do without gear or skills. Variety without decision fatigue."

### 15. Icebreaker Prompts
"In my profile, three short prompts: 'Ask me about: Bay Area hiking, coffee roasting, indie games.' Only visible to people in my events. Not a bio - just conversation starters for strangers meeting in person."

### 16. Host Privileges
"As host, I set the event time and saw who joined. I couldn't remove anyone - the system handles that through blacklists. But I could add a note: 'Bring water, trail is dry.' Hosts guide, they don't gatekeep."

### 17. Minimum Viable Group
"I created an event. One person joined. That was enough - the event was on. If no one had joined by 4 hours before start, the app would have quietly suggested I go solo instead."

### 18. Blacklist Invisibility
"I rated someone poorly. They vanished from my suggestions. Later, they hosted an event - I never saw it. I joined an event - they never saw it existed. We'll never cross paths again. The system routes around friction silently."

### 19. Always Rate
"Every event, every time: rate the experience, rate each person. Even the same trail on a different day. The system needs fresh signal - memories fade, contexts change."

### 20. Friend Notifications
"After mutual 5-stars, we were both asked if we wanted to be friends. We both accepted. Now when Sarah hosts an event, I see her name first. When she joins something, it bubbles up in my suggestions. Not surveillance - just a gentle nudge toward people I already trust."
